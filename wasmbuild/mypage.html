WasmGL

Canvas click toggles: orbit the camera with the mouse vs. keep a fixed view. Use the panel to add solids, move and resize the selected object, move the camera, and run CSG on two chosen objects.

1 · Add object

Choose type, set size, then add to the scene.

2 · Selected object

Pick what transforms and resizing apply to.

3 · Resize selection

Boxes use full width / height / depth. Spheres use radius and segment counts (mesh quality).

CSG results cannot be resized here — add a new primitive instead.

4 · Move selection object

Translate and rotate the selected mesh in world space (metres and degrees).

5 · Scene view camera

Move the camera (not the object): pan in view space, orbit, and zoom (field of view).

6 · Boolean CSG two objects

Pick operand A and B (different indices). Union and intersection are symmetric; difference is A − B.
